About Our Society

FAST Debating Society is a platform for students to express their opinions, improve their communication skills and develop confidence. We organize debates, competitions, public speaking sessions and discussions on interesting topics. Our goal is to create future leaders and confident speakers.




Joining Requirements

Year Requirements
1st Year Interest in debating and communication
2nd Year Basic speaking and presentation skills
3rd Year Previous debating experience
4th Year Leadership and mentoring skills
