Meet Our Team


Who We Are

FAST Debating Society is managed by a dedicated team of students who are passionate about debating, communication and leadership. Our team works together to organize events, guide members and create opportunities for students to develop their speaking abilities and confidence.




Our Leadership Team


Position Responsibilities
President Leads the society and manages its overall activities.
Vice President Assists the president and supervises team activities.
General Secretary Manages records, meetings and society communication.
Event Coordinator Plans and organizes debates and competitions.
Media Coordinator Manages announcements and event promotions.



One Team, One Voice!

Together, we work to create a community where every student gets the opportunity to speak, learn and grow.
