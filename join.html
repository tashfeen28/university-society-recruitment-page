Why Join Us?

Joining FAST Debating Society gives you an opportunity to express your ideas, improve your communication skills and develop confidence. Whether you are a beginner or an experienced speaker, our society welcomes students who are passionate about learning and sharing their opinions.




Joining Requirements


Year Requirements
1st Year Interest in debating and communication.
2nd Year Basic speaking and presentation skills.
3rd Year Previous debating experience.
4th Year Leadership and mentoring skills.
